## Where Reply Better AI is the better fit

**You write more than 15 replies a month.** That is Jetwriter's free limit. Reply Better AI has no cap.

**You want to pay nothing, or only for what you use.** The on-device, local and Groq engines are free. If you want a top model, bring your own OpenRouter key and pay per use instead of a $14-18 monthly plan.

**You want your text to stay on your computer.** With the on-device engine or your own Ollama / LM Studio server, nothing leaves your machine. There is no account and no analytics.

**You use Firefox.** Jetwriter lists Firefox as coming soon. Reply Better AI is already there.

## Where Jetwriter is the better fit

**Gmail auto-drafts.** Jetwriter can draft replies in Gmail ahead of time. Reply Better AI drafts only when you click.

**Reading tools.** Jetwriter can summarize whole pages and answer questions about them. Reply Better AI only summarizes the messages you select.

**One bill for top models.** If you don't want to manage an API key, Jetwriter's paid plans include advanced models.

## How to try it

1. Install Reply Better AI from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
2. Select the message you want to answer, or click into a box where you're writing.
3. Click the small button in the corner of the box and pick a style or tone.

On Chrome desktop it uses the free on-device engine automatically when available. Elsewhere, paste a free Groq key or connect a local model in settings.

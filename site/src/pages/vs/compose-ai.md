## Where Reply Better AI is the better fit

**You reply to more than 10 emails a month.** That is Compose AI's free limit, and its Premium plan stops at 50. Reply Better AI has no cap.

**You want replies in your own tone.** Pick match the thread, professional, friendly or concise, or type what you want to say in any language. Learn my voice can also pick up how you write.

**You want your text to stay on your computer.** With the on-device engine or your own Ollama / LM Studio server, nothing leaves your machine. There is no account and no analytics.

**You use Firefox.** Reply Better AI is on Firefox Add-ons too.

## Where Compose AI is the better fit

**Autocomplete.** Compose AI suggests the rest of your sentence as you type. Reply Better AI only works when you click.

**Writing from a prompt anywhere.** Compose AI lets you type `//` and describe what you want written. Reply Better AI is built around rewriting and replying to text that's already on the page.

## How to try it

1. Install Reply Better AI from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
2. Open an email, select the message you want to answer, and click the small button.
3. Pick a tone, check the draft, and insert it with one click.

On Chrome desktop it uses the free on-device engine automatically when available. Elsewhere, paste a free Groq key or connect a local model in settings.

## Where Reply Better AI is the better fit

**You rewrite more than 10 things a day.** Wordtune's free plan resets daily at 10 rewrites. Its middle plan raises that to 30. Reply Better AI has no daily or monthly cap.

**You answer a lot of messages.** Select the email or chat you're replying to, pick a tone, and get a full reply you can insert with one click. Wordtune focuses on rewording what you already wrote.

**You want your text to stay on your computer.** With the on-device engine or your own Ollama / LM Studio server, nothing leaves your machine. There is no account and no analytics.

**You use Firefox.** Reply Better AI is on Firefox Add-ons and kept up to date there.

## Where Wordtune is the better fit

**Spelling and grammar.** Wordtune flags mistakes and suggests fixes as part of every plan. Reply Better AI works on demand: you click when you want a rewrite.

**Mobile.** Wordtune has an iOS app. Reply Better AI is desktop-browser only.

**Summaries.** Wordtune can summarize articles and documents. Reply Better AI only summarizes the messages you select.

## How to try it

1. Install Reply Better AI from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
2. Click into any message box and start typing, or select a message you want to answer.
3. Click the small button in the corner of the box and pick a style or tone.

On Chrome desktop it uses the free on-device engine automatically when available. Elsewhere, paste a free Groq key or connect a local model in settings.

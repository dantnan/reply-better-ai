## Where Reply Better AI is the better fit

**You want AI rewrites without a quota.** Grammarly's free plan gives 100 AI prompts a month and does not include full-sentence rewrites. Reply Better AI has no monthly cap, and full rewrites are the core feature.

**You answer a lot of messages.** Select the email or chat you're replying to, pick a tone (match the thread, professional, friendly, concise), and get a reply you can insert with one click. You can also type what you want to say in any language.

**You don't want your writing on someone else's server.** With the on-device engine or your own Ollama / LM Studio server, your text never leaves your computer. There is no account, and the extension sends no analytics.

**You want something that stays out of the way.** The button only appears in real message boxes, not on every input on the page. There are no pop-ups or upsells inside the editor.

## Where Grammarly is the better fit

**Live typo and grammar checking.** Grammarly underlines mistakes as you type. Reply Better AI works on demand: you click when you want a rewrite.

**Google Docs, desktop apps and mobile.** Grammarly works in Google Docs and has desktop and mobile apps. Reply Better AI is a browser extension and doesn't support Google Docs yet.

**Team features.** If your company manages style guides and seats centrally, that is Grammarly's territory.

## How to try it

1. Install Reply Better AI from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
2. Click into any message box and start typing, or select a message you want to answer.
3. Click the small button in the corner of the box and pick a style or tone.

On Chrome desktop it uses the free on-device engine automatically when available. Elsewhere, paste a free Groq key or connect a local model in settings.
